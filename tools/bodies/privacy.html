Privacy

What we do with what you tell us

Short version: we use it to answer you, we do not sell it, and you can have it deleted by asking.

Only what you type into the enquiry form: your names, an email address, optionally a telephone number, your date, your church or diocese, a guest number, and whatever you write in the message box. Nothing else, and nothing you have not typed.

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and embeds no trackers. The only request it makes to another company is for its two typefaces, served by Google Fonts.

To reply to your enquiry and, if you engage us, to plan your wedding. That is the only purpose. Our lawful basis is your consent in sending the enquiry, and thereafter the performance of our contract with you.

Enquiries that do not become bookings are deleted within twelve months. Records of weddings we have planned are kept for six years, which is what our accountants and insurers require.

If you engage us, the suppliers your wedding actually needs — your parish, your venue, your musicians — and only the details each of them needs to do their job. Never a mailing list, never a data broker, and never anyone who has paid us for it, because nobody pays us for anything.

Under the GDPR you may ask us for a copy of everything we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to {{email}} and we will do it within a month, usually the same week.

If we get it wrong you may complain to the Data Protection Commission, the Irish supervisory authority.

This notice is written plainly rather than legally. Have it reviewed before launch if you want belt and braces — and update the retention periods above to whatever you actually do.
