The process

From first email to the last car home

Eight steps. You will always know which one you are in, and what we need from you next.

01

The first conversation

An hour, in person in Dublin or Galway, or by video. We ask about the two of you, your parishes, your families and your budget. No charge and no obligation on either side.

02

Proposal and fixed fee

Within a week: a written proposal, a scope, and a fixed fee. If we are not the right house for your wedding we will say so and suggest someone who is.

03

Church and date

Before anything else is booked. We approach the parish, establish what is possible, secure the date, and identify any permission or delegation the wedding will need.

04

The liturgy sessions

Two sittings, ideally with your celebrant present. We read the rite together and make every choice it offers you. You leave with the order of the rite on paper.

05

Design and suppliers

Now the flowers. Design direction for sanctuary, nave and reception as one idea, then a curated supplier list, tendered and contracted in your name.

06

Paperwork closed out

Baptismal and confirmation certificates, letters of freedom, the Accord certificate, any permissions or dispensations, and the State notice appointment. Closed by three months out.

07

Rehearsal week

The production schedule goes to every supplier, the worship aids are delivered, the musicians rehearse in the building, and we walk the rite with you and your families.

08

The day

We are in the sacristy from early. Two directors, one in the church and one at the venue. You will not be asked a single logistical question after nine in the morning.
